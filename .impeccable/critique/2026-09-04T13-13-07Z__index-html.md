## Overall Impression

The thinking is better than the surface. The honesty layer is the best thing here. The list is generic, the muted-text token fails contrast everywhere, and the two things a user does (switch universe, star a name) are the least-designed interactions. Biggest opportunity: make the home screen carry motion.
