## How to read the numbers

- **Capture** is the share of a perfect trader's profit that the crossover kept, net of costs. The perfect trader buys every valley and sells every peak of every name, where a peak or valley is a turn of at least 20% (the primary definition; 10% and 30% are shown for comparison). 100% would be perfect timing; a crossover loses to lag and to whipsaw. Buy-and-hold has a capture of its own, and beating it is the bar.
- **Entry premium** is how far above the valley the crossover bought, **exit discount** how far below the peak it sold, when it did signal. **Lag** is the days between the turn and the signal.
- **Held through / sat out** means the crossover never left before the valley (or never re-entered before the peak), so there was no signal to be late with. **Missed / rode down** means it stayed out for the whole rise, or stayed in for the whole fall.
- **Whipsaw per turn**: crosses that matched no real turn, per real turn. Zero is ideal.
- **Universe curve**: every member held under the crossover rule, equal weight, daily, against the same universe held outright.
