## How to read the numbers

- **IC** is the rank correlation, across all names on one week end, between the oscillator reading and the return that followed. It is averaged over all week ends.
- **Negative IC = mean reversion**: names reading stretched-high went on to lag, stretched-low went on to lead. Positive IC = continuation.
- **t** is the IC's t-statistic with the overlap correction. Beyond about ±2 is unlikely to be luck; beyond ±3 is solid. Same sign in train and test is the real test.
- **D1 / D10** are the mean forward returns of the lowest tenth of readings and the highest tenth, so an effect that lives only in the extremes still shows.
